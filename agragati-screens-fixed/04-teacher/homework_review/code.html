Topic 12: Bound States & Wavefunctions in Finite Square Wells

Candidate: Beatrice Laurent • Exam Session November 2024 / May 2025
Marks Weight: 20 pts Validated Authenticity
1. Time-Independent Schrödinger Equation Formulation QUESTION 4A

For a particle of mass m confined within a one-dimensional potential well of width L where V(x) = 0 for 0 < x < L and V(x) = V₀ elsewhere:

-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2\psi(x)}{dx^2} + V(x)\psi(x) = E\psi(x)

Imposing continuous boundary conditions at x = 0 and x = L yields the characteristic transcendental eigenvalue relation for bound states (E < V₀):

k \tan\left(\frac{k L}{2}\right) = \alpha \quad \text{where} \quad k = \frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar}, \; \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2m(V_0 - E)}}{\hbar}
