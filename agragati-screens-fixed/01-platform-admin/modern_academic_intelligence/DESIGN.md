## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of Ivy League academic gravitas, Swiss private banking discretion, and Series-B institutional intelligence. It is crafted explicitly for leadership, provosts, deans, and headmasters who demand executive oversight without the visual noise of transactional SaaS dashboards.

The visual narrative relies on an editorial discipline:
- **Atmosphere:** High-trust, intellectual, architectural, and definitive. Surfaces read like heavy-stock archival paper mounted on dark lacquered millwork.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Editorial Minimalism merged with high-precision enterprise data density. Visual drama is established through stark typographic scale contrast—pairing monumental, literary serifs with razor-sharp, utilitarian grotesque sans-serifs.
- **Discipline & Restraint:** Strictly no generic gradients, frosted glass, or playful skeuomorphism. Depth is produced via hairline borders, quiet tonal tiering, and micro-diffused ambient drop shadows tinted with navy ink.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical ink and parchment, punctuated with controlled antique gold for focal authority.

### Core Architectural Roles
- **Primary Ink-Navy (`#141F38`):** The institutional anchor. Used for the persistent primary navigation spine, critical headings, and foundational interactive structures.
- **Slate-Navy (`#26365C`):** Structural tertiary layer. Provides depth within dark surfaces, subtle hover states for dark navigation items, and secondary iconography.
- **Warm Antique Gold (`#C9A24B`):** Applied with deliberate restraint. Reserved exclusively for single primary executive actions, active indicator marks, key metric callouts, and bespoke analytical trends. Never used across large decorative surfaces.
- **Bronze-Gold (`#9C7A32`):** Accessible contrast pairing for gold text labels, numerical values, and micro-eyebrows resting on light paper backgrounds.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAF7`):** Warm unbleached off-white reminiscent of heavy archival book stock. Softens eye fatigue across long analytical sessions.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` to `#FDFCFA`):** Crisp, warm-white cards framed by hairline borders (`#E8E7E1` or `rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.08)`).

### Typography Tones
- **Ink-Charcoal (`#1C1E26`):** Deep editorial body black with soft warm undertones.
- **Muted Stone (`#6B6F76`):** Secondary editorial narrative and structural metadata.
- **Parchment Mist (`#9C9FA6`):** Subtle captions, disabled states, and table column heads.

### Institutional Semantic Indicators
All semantic indicators avoid neon saturation; they are rendered in museum-grade, subdued historical pigments:
- **Success / Active (`#6B8F71`):** Muted sage green.
- **Warning / Trial (`#C08A3E`):** Muted ochre amber.
- **Critical / Suspended (`#B4523F`):** Muted brick terracotta.

## Typography

The type system creates an explicit high-contrast hierarchy:
- **Headlines & Primary Metrics:** `Newsreader` delivers literary authority and editorial pacing. Numbers rendered in `kpi-metric` reference classic financial reports and institutional reviews. Italics are reserved exclusively for contextual nuance, quotes, or secondary descriptors in analytical headers.
- **Operational Interface:** `Public Sans` balances technical clarity with institutional neutrality. It provides uncompromised legibility within complex data matrices and dense parameter forms.
- **Eyebrows & Section Markers:** Strict small-caps formatting (`11px`, `0.08em` tracking) styled in Bronze-Gold (`#9C7A32`) or Muted Stone (`#6B6F76`) establishes instant departmental orientation without demanding dominant weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an intentional asymmetrical split: an anchor command sidebar coupled with an editorial multi-column workspace.

### Structural Framework
- **Primary Shell:** A fixed, full-height command column (`280px` / `17.5rem`) rendered in solid Ink-Navy (`#141F38`). 
- **Workspace Canvas:** A fluid, adaptive canvas with a maximum container threshold of `1408px` (`88rem`) to preserve comfortable reading lengths for data tables and editorial briefs.
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column grid using `24px` gutters on desktop displays (`≥1024px`), compressing to a 4-column system with `16px` margins on mobile viewports (`<768px`).

### Editorial Table Densities
Data tables do not use compact, overcrowded heights. Rows are given generous breathing room:
- **Standard Row Height:** `56px` minimum.
- **Featured / Parent Metric Rows:** `64px` to `72px` with interior cellular padding of `16px 20px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and grounded, rejecting synthetic neon glows or multi-tiered blurs.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat base parchment (`#FAFAF7`).
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** White panels (`#FFFFFF` to `#FDFCFA`) outlined with a crisp `1px` hairline border in `#E8E7E1` (or `rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.08)`). Backed by an ultra-diffused, navy-tinted ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Executive Flyouts & Dropdowns):** Subtle lift with defined border integrity: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.06), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Command Overlays & Modals):** Backdrop shielded by deep ink scrim: `rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.45)`. Panels present crisp `1px` borders paired with deep architectural shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature balances architectural stability with contemporary industrial precision:
- **Panels, Modals & Data Cards:** Fixed at `14px` border-radius. This subtle curve prevents the interface from feeling clinical while avoiding the playfulness of hyper-rounded corners.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Badges):** Standardized at `10px` for clear tactile affordance.
- **Status Indicators & Micro-Chips:** Scaled to `9999px` (pill form) when holding atomic contextual status (e.g., Active, Review, Archive).

## Components

### Command Sidebar
- **Appearance:** Solid `#141F38` background with an integrated hairline right border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Branding Header:** Elegant serif wordmark set in antique gold with an ascending hairline rule motif underneath.
- **Navigation Links:** `14px` Medium `Public Sans`. Passive links in `#9C9FA6`; active states switch to pure white text resting on a faint slate wash (`rgba(38, 54, 92, 0.45)`), demarcated by a solid `2px` Antique Gold vertical left rail.

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional CTA:** Antique Gold fill (`#C9A24B`), Ink-Navy text (`#141F38`), weight 600, `10px` radius. Hover shifts to `#B8923D`. Never used more than once per primary screen view.
- **Secondary Authority:** Ink-Navy fill (`#141F38`), Warm-White text (`#FAFAF7`), `10px` radius. Hover shifts to Slate-Navy (`#26365C`).
- **Outline / Editorial:** Crisp `1px` border in `#E8E7E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1C1E26`. Hover introduces faint warm canvas fill (`#FAFAF7`) and border tint (`#141F38`).

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height: `42px` uniform.
- Borders: `1px` solid `#E8E7E1` on pure white background.
- Focus: Immediate transition to `1px` solid `#141F38` with a subtle outer ring (`rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.08)` at `3px`). No default OS cyan or saturated blue highlights.

### Status Pills & Data Badges
- Constructed with a `10%` to `15%` tint fill paired with dark, high-legibility semantic text:
  - **Active / Enrolled:** Background `rgba(107, 143, 113, 0.12)`, text `#3D5B42`.
  - **Trial / Pending:** Background `rgba(192, 138, 62, 0.12)`, text `#7A521E`.
  - **Suspended / At-Risk:** Background `rgba(180, 82, 63, 0.12)`, text `#752D20`.
- Padding: `4px 10px`, typography: `11px` bold uppercase with `0.04em` tracking.

### Editorial Data Tables
- Header: `#FAFAF7` background, `44px` height, `11px` uppercase stone-gray labels with `0.08em` tracking. Bottom border: `1px` solid `#E8E7E1`.
- Rows: `56px` minimum height, pure white surface alternating to `#FDFCFA` on hover. Interior cells bordered by razor-thin `1px` rules (`rgba(20, 31, 56, 0.05)`).
